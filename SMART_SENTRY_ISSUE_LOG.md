| 2026-04-10 | **Partial-Frame Driver Degradation** — After a prolonged camera run, a USB webcam (MSMF/DSHOW) can enter a degraded state where the driver continues delivering full-size frame buffers (e.g. 1920×1080) but only populates the upper-left sub-rectangle with actual video data; the remainder stays zero-filled black. The existing blackout detector missed this because the frame is not *entirely* black — the upper-left content raises the mean above the blackout threshold. This caused the video panel to show the camera feed squished into the upper-left corner with the crosshair and HUD centered on the full (mostly black) frame. The condition typically follows the black-frame / RGB-artifact glitch sequence that was partially fixed on 2026-04-09. | Added `_is_probable_partial_frame()` detector in `app/sentry_v2/sentry_v2_tab.py` that samples top-left vs bottom-right quadrants to detect content-only-in-corner frames. Extended `_handle_camera_frame_health()` to count consecutive partial frames and trigger camera recovery after 6 occurrences. While recovery is pending, `_queue_local_frame()` crops partial frames to their content area so the operator still sees usable video. Added `_camera_partial_frame_count` counter, reset it in `_close_camera()`, and exported it in runtime snapshots. | Worked |
